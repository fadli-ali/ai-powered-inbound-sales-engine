# Live Demo Guide

## 60–90 Second Walkthrough

### 1. Frame the business problem

“I built this prototype to reduce repetitive inbound lead triage while keeping qualification transparent and outbound communication human-controlled.”

### 2. Show the intake form

Open the Tally form and point out the fields used for qualification: event size, timeline, requested capabilities, and the prospect's main challenge.

### 3. Submit the Avery scenario

- Avery Chen
- avery.chen@example.com
- Northstar Events (fictional)
- 500–1,999 attendees
- Within 3 months
- Registration and ticketing
- On-site check-in
- Reporting and analytics
- “Our team manages registration in spreadsheets, and check-in lines were too long at our last conference. We need a clearer view of attendance.”

### 4. Show the automation

Open the active Make scenario and briefly explain that it captures the response, records time, calls OpenAI, validates the JSON, and creates the HubSpot outputs.

### 5. Show the CRM result

Open Avery's HubSpot contact and highlight:

- High priority, 7/8
- Transparent point-based reasoning
- Lead summary and customer needs
- Discovery questions
- Personalized draft marked for human review

### 6. Show the control

Open the associated email task and emphasize: “The system prepares the work, but it does not send anything automatically. A salesperson must review, edit, and approve the draft.”

### 7. Close with validation

Briefly mention that Jordan produced Medium, 4/8 and Casey produced Low, 0/8, demonstrating all three qualification bands.

## Suggested Closing Statement

“This prototype shows how I approach business automation: start with a clear operational problem, make the decision logic visible, integrate the tools people already use, preserve human judgment, and measure what actually happened.”
