# Qualification Model

I designed the qualification model to be simple enough for a salesperson to explain and audit from the information a prospect submits.

## Scoring Inputs

### Event size: 0–3 points

| Response | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| 2,000+ | 3 | Largest operational scope in the prototype |
| 500–1,999 | 2 | Substantial event complexity |
| 100–499 | 1 | Moderate event scope |
| Under 100 / Not sure | 0 | Smaller or undefined opportunity |

### Timeline: 0–3 points

| Response | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Within 3 months | 3 | Near-term need requiring timely discovery |
| 4–6 months | 2 | Defined opportunity with planning time |
| 7–12 months | 1 | Longer-term opportunity |
| More than 12 months / Date undecided | 0 | Low immediate urgency |

### Requirements: 0–2 points

| Response | Points | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Three or more capabilities | 2 | Broad, clearly expressed requirements |
| One or two capabilities | 1 | Some defined product interest |
| Only “Not sure yet” | 0 | Requirements need early-stage exploration |

## Priority Thresholds

| Score | Classification | Intended action |
|---:|---|---|
| 6–8 | High | Prompt review and discovery preparation |
| 3–5 | Medium | Organized follow-up with normal urgency |
| 0–2 | Low | Nurture-oriented discovery without overstating urgency |

## Guardrails

- The rules are prototype-defined and do not represent an employer's internal sales methodology.
- The score prioritizes operational follow-up; it does not predict revenue or conversion.
- AI explains the result, but the underlying point values remain visible.
- A salesperson reviews the record and draft before external communication.
